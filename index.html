About Dewey

Celebrating 98 years of pest-free California

Dewey Pest Control was founded by Ray M. Dewey in 1929. What began as a one-man operation has grown into an organization of hundreds of technicians, crew workers, inspectors, fumigators, and office personnel — all dedicated to keeping you pest free.

Our Integrated Pest Management approach means we treat the problem at its source, with products proven earth friendly and safe for your family and pets.

  • Dedicated — if a problem comes back, we return, no questions asked
  • Flexible — we work within your schedule
  • Green — earth-friendly products, pet and child friendly
  • Free initial consultation, no obligation
